Why SmartHarvest?

Everything You Need for Smart Crop Storage

From real-time IoT monitoring to automated ventilation — built for farmers, by technologists.

Temperature Monitoring

Real-time temperature tracking with historical graphs and crop-specific threshold alerts to prevent spoilage.

Humidity Control

Monitor relative humidity and get notified when moisture levels deviate from the safe range for your crop type.

Gas Level Detection

Raw gas sensor readings help detect early signs of fermentation, spoilage, or unsafe storage conditions.

Ventilation Automation

Smart fan control with Auto and Manual modes. Set thresholds and let the system manage airflow automatically.

Live Analytics

Beautiful interactive Chart.js dashboards showing historical trends and current sensor values for each slot.

Instant Alerts

Configurable per-crop notifications for temperature spikes, humidity extremes, device disconnection, and more.

How It Works

Three simple steps to protect your harvest with smart IoT technology.

1

Register & Book

Create your account, choose a crop category, pick a storage slot, and confirm your booking online.

2

Store Your Crops

Place your produce in the smart storage unit. IoT sensors begin monitoring conditions immediately.

3

Monitor in Real-Time

Track live sensor data, receive alerts, control the ventilation fan, and view historical graphs anytime.
